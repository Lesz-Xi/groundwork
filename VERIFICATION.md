## Limits

This is author QA, not independent review, full accessibility certification, scientific validation or trademark clearance. Mobile layouts were viewport/touch-emulated, not tested on physical devices. Safari/iOS, Firefox, screen readers and physical printers have not been certified. The enlarged-text fixture is not native browser-zoom certification. External citations were not re-fetched for publication; their recorded source-reading scope remains the guide's stated boundary.
